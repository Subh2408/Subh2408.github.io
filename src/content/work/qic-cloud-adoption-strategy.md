QIC's enterprise cloud agreement had sat unformalised for about 18 months before I
took it on. From my first round to signature took about four months. Closing it was
commercial work as much as technical work.

## Reading the spend

I started by breaking the spend down and analysing its trend. That showed where
the money was going and where it wasn't. Usage was growing with the AI workloads.

## Sizing the commitment

A committed-use agreement trades a spend commitment for a discount. The question is
how much to commit. I forecast where spend was heading and sized the commitment to
that forecast, rather than to current spend. That is the judgement the deal rests on.

## Getting it signed

The agreement went through three to four negotiation rounds. IT, finance and
procurement had to agree before it could be signed. So did the data and AI teams,
and the provider's account team.

## Outcome

QIC signed the contract in January 2026. It carries committed-use discounts and delivered
significant savings.
