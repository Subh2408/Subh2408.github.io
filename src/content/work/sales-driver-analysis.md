Led a high-impact sales driver analysis for a **leading US-based feminine care brand (Fortune 100 FMCG)**, providing deep insights into the levers affecting volume sales across major North American big-box retailers. The comprehensive analysis spanned core product segments (Pads, Tampons, Liners) and quantified the impact of marketing, pricing, distribution, and promotional activities.

This project equipped the client with a statistically rigorous framework to optimise commercial strategies, enhance internal alignment, and ultimately led to an **expanded scope of work, securing additional contracts valued at over $100K**.

---

## The Challenge: Decoding In-Store Sales Performance

The client sought to deconstruct the drivers of their feminine care portfolio's in-store performance. Lacking a unified, data-backed understanding of how different commercial levers influenced sales across various product segments and retail channels, they needed a robust analytical framework to:

-   Identify key drivers of volume sales at a granular level.
-   Differentiate these drivers across major retail partners.
-   Quantify the contribution of media, price, distribution, and promotions.
-   Guide strategic resource allocation for marketing and sales efforts.

---

## Strategic Analytical Approach

Our approach focused on analysing **volume sales** (to isolate true consumer demand from pricing effects) using Nielsen sales data, encompassing:

-   **Core Data Inputs:** Systematically structured data across distribution metrics, pricing variables, promotional activity, and media investment (including adstock modelling to capture advertising carryover).
-   **Rigorous Modelling by Segment & Retailer:** Developed statistical models (e.g., log-log linear regression chosen for its interpretability and fit) for each product segment within each key retailer. This allowed for nuanced insights tailored to specific market conditions.
-   **Statistical Validation:** Ensured model robustness through standard diagnostic checks and validation procedures.
-   **Cross-Sectional Analysis:** Conducted aggregate analyses across all retailers (by segment) and across all segments (by retailer) to identify both universal and channel-specific growth levers.

---

## Key Insights & Deliverables

The analysis provided the client with clear, actionable intelligence:

-   **Granular Understanding of Sales Drivers:** Quantified the impact of distribution, pricing, media spend, and promotions on volume sales, differentiated by product segment and retail partner.
-   **Optimised Media Allocation Insights:** Adstock analysis provided a clearer picture of advertising effectiveness and informed more efficient media budget planning.
-   **Actionable Strategic Recommendations:** Delivered prioritised recommendations for optimising commercial levers to drive growth.
-   **Scalable Analytical Framework:** Provided a reusable modelling template for future analyses across other product categories or regions.
